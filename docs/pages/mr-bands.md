# MR bands (`/analysis/mr-bands`)

How players differ by skill band. Bands and the Japan adjustment are defined in [analysis-methods.md](../analysis-methods.md).
- **Band picker:** compare two bands side by side, or view all bands as columns.
- **Per-game rates** (median, quartiles, n): throws, techs, anti-airs, counter hits taken and landed, raw Drive Rushes, burnouts, perfect parries, after-block and wake-up attack %.
- **Spectra:** distribution of each axis per band (how often a Diamond player is Impulsive on defense vs a 2000+ player).
- **Archetype mix** per band.
- **Damage taken:** lens and situation shares per band (do lower bands lose more to jumping, higher bands to strike/throw?).
- **Drive economy** per band.
- **Biggest differences:** an auto-ranked list of the metrics that change most between neighbouring bands, with sample sizes.

Diamond and below is one band. MR unknown players are excluded, and the page says how many were left out.
